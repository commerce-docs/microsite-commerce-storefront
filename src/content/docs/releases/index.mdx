## What is a suite release?

A suite release marks a point in time when we have tested the storefront and its drop-in versions end-to-end. It serves as a quality starting point for new implementations. If you have already scaffolded or cloned the storefront, you do not need to "upgrade" to new suite releases — your codebase is your own. Suite releases are not packages and cannot be installed or updated as dependencies. The only dependencies to actively update are your drop-in packages (`@dropins/*`, `@adobe/*`), which follow semantic versioning. Minor and patch versions are non-breaking by contract. Use `npm outdated` to check for available updates.
