<Aside type="note" title="What is a suite release?">
A suite release marks a point in time when we have tested the storefront and its drop-in versions end-to-end. It serves as a quality starting point for new implementations. If you have already scaffolded or cloned the storefront, you do not need to "upgrade" to new suite releases — your codebase is your own. Suite releases are not packages and cannot be installed or updated as dependencies. The only dependencies to actively update are your drop-in packages (`@dropins/*`, `@adobe/*`), which follow semantic versioning. Minor and patch versions are non-breaking by contract. Use `npm outdated` to check for available updates.
</Aside>

## Release suites

Each release suite has its own page with highlights, breaking changes, updated components, and compatibility details. To see every change to a specific drop-in or package across releases, go to the [changelog](/releases/changelog/) instead.

- [July 2026 suite](/releases/2026-07/): Upgrades the Drop-in SDK to TypeScript 6.0 and ESLint 9, with accessibility fixes across drop-ins and Requisition List enhancements for B2B.
- [June 2026 suite](/releases/2026-06/): Introduces seller-assisted buying, B2B company hierarchy management, requisition list sharing, and reactive PDP recommendations.
- [April 2026 suite](/releases/2026-04/): Adds stored payment methods in My Account, storefront-visible order comments, and Product Recommendations v4 with catalog view support.
- [March 2026 suite](/releases/2026-03/): Adds product details page video support, multi-store wishlist, checkout shipping customization, and Quick Order for B2B.
- [February 2026 suite](/releases/2026-02/): Adds Document Authoring improvements, a Universal Editor model v2 upgrade, and drop-in updates for B2C and B2B.
- [January 2026 suite](/releases/2026-01/): Upgrades all B2C drop-ins to v3.0.0 and introduces five new B2B drop-in components for company management, purchase orders, quotes, and requisition lists.
- [October 2025 suite](/releases/2025-10/): Adds gift card product support, tiered pricing in Cart, and bundle product support in Wishlist and Product Details Page.
- [August 2025 suite](/releases/2025-08/): Adds cart item removal undo, a multi-step checkout template, and the Product Discovery 2.0 event-driven architecture.
- [June 2025 suite](/releases/2025-06/): Introduces four new drop-in components — Product Discovery, Product Recommendations, Wishlist, and Personalization — and upgrades the boilerplate to Helix 5.
- [April 2025 suite](/releases/2025-04/): Compatibility and drop-in package versions for this release suite.
- [December 2024 suite](/releases/2024-12/): Compatibility and drop-in package versions for this release suite.
