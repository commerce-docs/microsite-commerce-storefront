The Company drop-in component provides comprehensive B2B company management functionality for Adobe Commerce storefronts. It enables B2B customers to view and manage their company profile information, including company details, legal address, contacts, and payment/shipping methods. The component is permissions-aware, ensuring users can only access and modify information based on their assigned company role.
