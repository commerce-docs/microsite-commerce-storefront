Drop-ins for B2B are pre-built, customizable UI components that provide complete B2B commerce functionality for your storefront. Each drop-in handles specific B2B capabilities, from managing company accounts and organizational structures to handling purchase orders and negotiable quotes.

<TableWrapper nowrap={[0]}>

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Checkout overview](/dropins-b2b/checkout/) | Provides B2B-specific payment method containers for Payment on Account and Purchase Order, enabling company buyers to make purchases using approved credit limits and reference numbers. |
| [Company Management overview](/dropins-b2b/company-management/) | Enables B2B administrators and users to manage company accounts, organizational structures, roles, permissions, and credit. |
| [Company Switcher overview](/dropins-b2b/company-switcher/) | Enables B2B users with access to multiple companies to switch between company contexts, ensuring proper permissions and data isolation. |
| [Purchase Order overview](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/) | Enables full purchase order functionality, including purchase order creation, approval rules, conditional checkout logic, purchase order lists, and purchase order details. |
| [Quote Management overview](/dropins-b2b/quote-management/) | Enables requesting negotiable quotes, quote management and tracking, quote status updates, and quote comments and attachments. |
| [Requisition List overview](/dropins-b2b/requisition-list/) | Enables B2B buyers to create and manage organized lists of products for repeat purchasing, streamlining reorder workflows and simplifying procurement processes. |

</TableWrapper>
