## Overview

The **Company Management** drop-in provides **7** pre-built container components for integrating into your storefront.

<strong>Version: 1.0.0-beta16</strong>
</div>

## What are Containers?

Containers are pre-built UI components that combine functionality, state management, and presentation. They provide a complete solution for specific features and can be customized through props, slots, and CSS.

- [Company credit](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-credit/)
