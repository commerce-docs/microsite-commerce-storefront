Prepare your Adobe Commerce storefront on Edge Delivery Services (EDS) for production traffic. Complete the applicable preparation and staging checks before you switch traffic to the production site. Verify the production hostname during the switch, then complete the post-launch checks.

Record your staging results and launch sequence, including DNS, CDN, service, and content changes. Test your rollback plan so your team can restore the previous site if needed.

## When your launch includes Adobe Commerce Optimizer

If your business-to-consumer (B2C) storefront uses Adobe Commerce Optimizer with an existing Adobe Commerce on Cloud instance, start with the <Link href={"https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/optimizer/launch/launch-checklist"} text={"Adobe Commerce Optimizer launch checklist"} />. Use it for Cloud setup, Optimizer data ingestion, integrations, and program-wide launch checks. Then complete the applicable storefront checks below.

The shared storefront checks below adapt recommendations from that guide and the Edge Delivery Services go-live checklist. Apply only the checks that match your backend and integrations.

## Content and authoring

<Checklist checklistKey="content">

* [ ] Review the <Link href={"https://www.aem.live/docs/go-live-checklist"} text={"Edge Delivery Services go-live checklist"} /> and complete the checks that apply to your project.
* [ ] If you use document-based authoring, verify that your authoring source is configured correctly.
* [ ] Preview and publish your content, then verify that it appears as expected.
* [ ] Complete content and design quality assurance (QA) on your project's `.aem.live` domain.
* [ ] Verify that your favicon appears in the browser tab.
* [ ] If your content lives in SharePoint, configure dedicated access so Edge Delivery Services can read your source content. Manage author editing permissions separately. See <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/setup-customer-sharepoint" text="Dedicated SharePoint access" />.
* [ ] Test the drop-ins you use, such as cart, checkout, product details, product listing, sign-in, and account. Verify that your customizations work as expected. See the [Drop-ins introduction](/dropins/all/introduction/).
* [ ] Verify that your Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) design tokens, typography, and colors match your storefront branding. See [Customize blocks](/blocks/customize-blocks/).
</Checklist>

## SEO and indexing

<Checklist checklistKey="seo">

* [ ] Add document title metadata for key pages, especially product detail pages (PDPs) and product listing pages (PLPs). See [Search engine optimization (SEO) metadata](/setup/seo/metadata/).
* [ ] Confirm that your PDPs include [metadata and structured data](/setup/seo/metadata/), such as JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data (JSON-LD).
* [ ] Standardize URL formats for products (for example, `domain/product-name`).
* [ ] Redirect all vanity URLs to canonical URLs.
* [ ] Confirm that canonical URLs return `2xx` status codes (not `3xx` or `4xx`).
* [ ] Verify that `robots.txt` references your sitemaps and allows crawling of public content. For pages that must stay out of search results, allow crawling and add a `noindex` robots tag. Disallow rules alone do not prevent indexing. See <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/go-live-checklist#sitemap--robots" text="Sitemap and robots guidance" />.
* [ ] Configure the `redirects` document in the root of your project folder so URLs changed during migration still work, such as URLs that previously used the `.html` extension. For wildcard redirects, use CDN rules. See <Link href={"https://www.aem.live/docs/redirects"} text={"Redirects"} />.
* [ ] Generate a sitemap for your site and catalog. Submit it to Google Search Console as needed. For storefront indexing patterns, including multilingual setups, see [Indexing](/setup/seo/indexing/).
* [ ] For multilingual sites, include `hreflang` tags in the sitemap.
* [ ] Review the Google Search Console Page indexing report. Fix unexpected indexing problems affecting pages you want indexed, and confirm that intentional exclusions are expected.
</Checklist>

## Enable pre-rendering

<Checklist checklistKey="prerendering">

* [ ] Turn on pre-rendering for the pages that matter most at launch. See [Pre-rendering](/setup/configuration/aem-prerender/). Use [Server-side rendering (SSR) and crawlability](/setup/seo/ssr-and-crawlability/) to decide which pages need it.
* [ ] Use lowercase Edge Delivery Services route paths.
* [ ] View the source of a pre-rendered page. Confirm that metadata and main body content appear in the initial HTML response.
* [ ] For each language or region you support, open a sample page and confirm the translated content loads.
* [ ] Configure any additional HTML markup your project needs.
</Checklist>

## Performance and monitoring

<Checklist checklistKey="performance">

* [ ] Follow [Performance best practices](/get-started/performance/) for your storefront.
* [ ] If your project uses Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager, verify their configuration.
* [ ] Validate your <Link href={"https://github.com/adobe/commerce-events/tree/main/examples/events/snowplow-debugger"} text={"storefront events"} /> implementation. For Live Search or Product Recommendations, confirm that data appears in the corresponding dashboards in the Adobe Commerce Admin.
* [ ] Confirm that `analytics.environment` in your [storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#analytics) is set to `Testing` during development. Change it to `Production` at launch. See [Analytics instrumentation](/setup/analytics/instrumentation/).
* [ ] Verify that Lighthouse scores meet your project's targets on key pages. See [Performance best practices](/get-started/performance/).
</Checklist>

## Analytics and monitoring

<Checklist checklistKey="analytics">

* [ ] Confirm that Operational Telemetry is enabled before launch so you can compare performance before and after switching traffic. This feature is also called Real User Monitoring (RUM). See <Link href={"https://www.aem.live/docs/operational-telemetry"} text={"Operational Telemetry"} />. For implementation details, see <Link href={"https://www.aem.live/developer/operational-telemetry"} text={"Operational Telemetry developer documentation"} />. If you proxy RUM through your origin, follow [CDN configuration](/setup/configuration/content-delivery-network/#proxy-rum-through-the-origin-to-avoid-a-tls-handshake).
* [ ] If you use Adobe Experience Platform, confirm data collection is configured for the production site. See [Adobe Experience Platform](/setup/analytics/adobe-experience-platform/) for storefront credentials, datastreams, and how events reach the Edge Network.
* [ ] On the production hostname, confirm that marketing technology (MarTech) tags fire and send data to the right tools. For the Adobe Client Data Layer, storefront events, and Commerce analytics settings, see [Analytics instrumentation](/setup/analytics/instrumentation/) and the [analytics section of Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#analytics).
* [ ] Record analytics baselines, such as page views and bounce rate, to compare launch results. Separately, record Web Vitals and Lighthouse baselines using [Performance best practices](/get-started/performance/) to compare site speed.
* [ ] Complete a test order, from adding a product to the cart through checkout and order confirmation. Confirm that each step is tracked. See [Validation and testing](/setup/analytics/instrumentation/#validation-and-testing) and [Event collection and validation](/setup/analytics/instrumentation/#event-collection-and-validation). For a structured view of storefront events, use the <Link href={"https://github.com/adobe/commerce-events/tree/main/examples/events/snowplow-debugger"} text={"Snowplow debugger example"} />.
</Checklist>

## Security and access

<Checklist checklistKey="security">

* [ ] Restrict Document Authoring (DA.live) content editing to authorized authors. Separately, restrict Edge Delivery Services publishing to authorized publishers. See <Link href={"https://da.live/docs/administration/permissions"} text={"DA.live permissions"} /> and <Link href={"https://www.aem.live/docs/authentication-setup-authoring"} text={"Edge Delivery Services authentication setup for authoring"} />.
* [ ] If your project uses Product Visuals or AEM Assets, verify the required entitlements and integration setup for your deployment. See the <Link href={"https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/aem-assets-integration/overview"} text={"AEM Assets Integration for Adobe Commerce guide"} /> on Adobe Experience League. To enable AEM Assets images in your storefront configuration, see [AEM Assets integration](/setup/configuration/aem-assets-configuration/).
* [ ] Update password reset links in email templates so they match your Edge Delivery Services URLs. See the [FAQ](/troubleshooting/faq#what-should-i-do-if-my-email-template-links-are-broken-after-migrating-to-edge-delivery-services-or-helix).
* [ ] Verify production credentials for each integration and payment provider. Keep private keys and secrets out of public storefront configuration.
* [ ] Add your production domains to allowlists where your backends require it, and send a test webhook to confirm delivery.
* [ ] If your project uses cross-origin requests, restrict Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) to the origins your storefront and tools use. See [CORS setup](/setup/configuration/cors-setup/).
* [ ] Publish a privacy policy and a cookie consent flow that match your regions, including General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) rules when they apply.
</Checklist>

## CDN and caching

<Checklist checklistKey="cdn">

* [ ] Point your production CDN's content origin to your site's main-branch `.aem.live` origin, not a GraphQL endpoint. Configure GraphQL proxy routes separately if your CDN uses them. Verify that Sidekick extensions, sitemap jobs, and the image importer use the appropriate production service endpoints.

      * For Adobe Commerce on Cloud or on-premises, this is typically `commerce-core-endpoint`, for example, `https://yourstore.example.com/graphql`. A separate `commerce-endpoint` may handle catalog reads through Catalog Service.
      * For Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service, see [Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/) for backend-specific endpoint settings.
      * For Adobe Commerce Optimizer with an existing Adobe Commerce on Cloud instance, set `commerce-endpoint` to the production Optimizer GraphQL endpoint and `commerce-core-endpoint` to the Cloud GraphQL endpoint. If API Mesh proxies either service, use its endpoint for that service. See the <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/optimizer/launch/launch-checklist" text="Adobe Commerce Optimizer launch checklist" />.
* [ ] If you use Adobe Commerce Fastly, confirm that a valid CDN purge token is available for the production service. Set `authToken` and `serviceId` in your <Link href={"https://tools.aem.live/tools/cdn-setup/index.html"} text={"site configuration"} />.
* [ ] Test your [CDN configuration](/setup/configuration/content-delivery-network/) and confirm cache hits, manual purges, and automatic invalidation behave as you expect.
* [ ] Publish a small content change and verify that it appears on the production domain without stale HTML.
* [ ] For [multi-store setups](/setup/seo/indexing/#multi-store-setups), verify that cached Catalog Service and Live Search responses stay separate for each store. The default storefront adds a store-specific query parameter to request URLs. If you use a CDN cache rule instead, verify that its cache key distinguishes store contexts.
* [ ] If you lower DNS time-to-live (TTL) values for the transition, restore the original longer values when updating the DNS records for go-live. See <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/byo-cdn-adobe-managed" text="DNS TTL guidance" />.
* [ ] Confirm that DNS A and CNAME records for every hostname point to the right targets.
* [ ] Confirm that the SSL/TLS certificate is provisioned and verified for the production domain. Confirm that HTTPS is enforced everywhere.
* [ ] Confirm that visitors who type the apex domain and visitors who type `www` both land on the URL you want.
</Checklist>

## Catalog service

<Checklist checklistKey="catalog">

* [ ] Set your storefront's catalog endpoint using the backend-specific settings in [Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/).

      * For Adobe Commerce on Cloud or on-premises, switch `commerce-endpoint` to `https://catalog-service.adobe.io/graphql`. If the Adobe Commerce Optimizer Connector is configured for your project, use the Adobe Commerce Optimizer GraphQL endpoint or the API Mesh endpoint that proxies it.
      * For Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service, see [Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/) for backend-specific endpoint settings.
      * For Adobe Commerce Optimizer, use the production Optimizer GraphQL endpoint or the API Mesh endpoint that proxies it.
* [ ] If you use Adobe Commerce Optimizer, verify that `adobe-commerce-optimizer` is set to `true`. Verify that `commerce-endpoint` points to the production Optimizer GraphQL endpoint or its API Mesh endpoint, and that the catalog-view header contains your production catalog view ID. For a private catalog view, verify that the required `AC-Catalog-View-Access-Token` is valid. Confirm that the restricted access key has not expired and that someone is responsible for rotating it. See [Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/) and <Link href="https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/services/optimizer/merchandising-services/using-the-api/" text="Merchandising API authentication requirements" />.
* [ ] For Adobe Commerce on Cloud or on-premises projects that use the Commerce Services Connector, select your production SaaS project and data space. See <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/user-guides/integration-services/saas#createsaasenv" text="Select or create a SaaS project" />.
* [ ] Verify that production catalog data is synchronized to the production environment.
* [ ] For Adobe Commerce on Cloud or on-premises projects that use the Commerce Services Connector, obtain the production API key pair from the Commerce license owner. Use the public key as `x-api-key`; store the private key securely. See <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/user-guides/integration-services/saas#apikey" text="Commerce Services Connector credentials" />.
* [ ] Verify that each category page is configured for the intended category and displays the expected products.
* [ ] For Commerce Services Connector projects, verify that `headers.cs.Magento-Environment-Id` and `analytics.environment-id` in your storefront configuration contain your production SaaS data space ID. Verify that `headers.cs.x-api-key` contains the production public API key. See [Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/).
</Checklist>

## Testing

The <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/optimizer/launch/launch-checklist" text="Adobe Commerce Optimizer launch checklist" /> provides the testing recommendations below for B2C storefronts that combine Adobe Commerce Optimizer, Edge Delivery Services, and an existing Adobe Commerce on Cloud instance. Catalog Service has a separate load-testing restriction, linked below.

<Checklist checklistKey="testing">

* [ ] Test core flows end to end, including browsing, search, filtering, cart, checkout, and account creation.
* [ ] Verify that payment gateways accept real and test transactions.
* [ ] Test order placement, confirmation email, and order tracking.
* [ ] Verify that shipping options and tax calculations are accurate.
* [ ] Test coupons, discounts, and loyalty programs that your project uses.
* [ ] Complete user acceptance testing (UAT) on staging and production.
* [ ] Complete load and stress testing and share the results with your assigned Adobe Customer Technical Advisor (CTA) or Customer Success Engineer (CSE). For Catalog Service, use the sandbox endpoint and submit a support ticket before testing. See <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/catalog-service/installation" text="Catalog Service load-testing requirements" />.
* [ ] Verify desktop and mobile performance against your project's targets. For launches covered by the <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/optimizer/launch/launch-checklist" text="Adobe Commerce Optimizer launch checklist" />, confirm that page load time is less than three seconds.
* [ ] Verify that images, scripts, and other assets are optimized.
* [ ] Test Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge for consistent behavior.
* [ ] Test responsive layouts on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
* [ ] Test performance on 3G, 4G, and Wi-Fi connections.
* [ ] Complete an accessibility audit against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), including screen reader and keyboard navigation testing.
* [ ] Establish a post-launch 404 monitoring plan.
* [ ] Test your rollback plan to verify that you can restore the previous site.
</Checklist>

## Launch day and post-launch

<Checklist checklistKey="management">

* [ ] For Adobe Commerce on Cloud projects, notify your assigned CTA, CSE, and Account Manager (AM) at least four weeks before launch. See the <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce-learn/tutorials/adobe-commerce-cloud/commerce-cloud-best-practices/pre-launch-checklist" text="Cloud pre-launch checklist" />.
* [ ] Record the priority 1 (P1) support hotline number: US toll-free (+1) 800-497-0335. Verify your region's number and the current menu option in <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/support-resources/adobe-support-tools-guide/adobe-commerce-support/adobe-commerce-p1-notification-hotline" text="Adobe Commerce P1 hotline numbers" />.
* [ ] Train your team to open a support ticket before calling the <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/support-resources/adobe-support-tools-guide/adobe-commerce-support/adobe-commerce-p1-notification-hotline" text="Adobe Commerce P1 hotline" />.
* [ ] Check for the latest storefront updates and update your project as needed. See [Monitor storefront changes](/boilerplate/updates/#monitor-boilerplate-changes).
* [ ] Verify that your disaster recovery plan has been tested.
* [ ] After launch, verify Lighthouse scores on the production domain.
* [ ] After launch, monitor Google Search Console for indexing and crawl errors.
* [ ] After launch, monitor 404 reports and add redirects for high-traffic legacy URLs.
* [ ] After launch, verify that production marketing tags send data to the intended analytics tools.
* [ ] For Adobe Commerce on cloud infrastructure Pro projects, ask your CTA, CSE, or AM whether high-SLA (service-level agreement) availability monitoring applies and confirm which URL it monitors. See <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/support-resources/adobe-support-tools-guide/adobe-commerce-support/monitoring-whitepaper" text="Adobe Commerce monitoring" />.
* [ ] Establish a process to keep your storefront and extension packages up to date. Start with [Updates](/boilerplate/updates/) and [Release information](/releases/).
</Checklist>
