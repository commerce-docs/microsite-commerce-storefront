## Stay current

Adobe updates the storefront platform on a regular release cadence. To stay informed:

- **Watch** the [Commerce boilerplate repository](https://github.com/hlxsites/aem-boilerplate-commerce) on GitHub for boilerplate releases.
- **Subscribe** to Adobe Commerce release announcements through the [Adobe Experience League](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce).
- **Check** the [Boilerplate updates](/boilerplate/updates/) page before upgrading your `@dropins/tools` version.
