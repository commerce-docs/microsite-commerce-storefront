The `ProductRating` container shows a product's average star rating and approved review count on the product details page, for example below the product name. It uses the SDK `StarRating` component, so it matches the ratings on product listing and recommendation cards.

The rating comes from two Catalog Service product attributes: `ac_rating_summary`, the average rating from `0` to `100`, and `ac_review_count`, the number of approved reviews. The container divides `ac_rating_summary` by 20 to show 0–5 stars. For how these attributes reach Catalog Service, see the [Ratings and Reviews overview](/dropins/ratings-reviews/#how-it-works).

Like other Product Details containers, `ProductRating` receives initial product data during [initialization](/dropins/product-details/initialization/) and updates with data emitted to `pdp/data` within its event scope.

## When the rating appears

The container renders nothing unless all of these are true:

- Both `ac_rating_summary` and `ac_review_count` are present in the product data with valid numbers.
- Both attributes have the `visible_in_pdp` storefront role. The Product Details drop-in keeps only product attributes with this role.
- The product has at least one approved review.

For configurable products, the container keeps the parent product's rating when the shopper selects options. Review summaries belong to the parent product, not to its variants.

The container also accepts standard `div` attributes, such as `className`.

## Example

The following example renders `ProductRating` below `ProductHeader` in the product details block:

```js title="blocks/product-details/product-details.js"
import { render as pdpRendered } from '@dropins/storefront-pdp/render.js';
import ProductHeader from '@dropins/storefront-pdp/containers/ProductHeader.js';
import ProductRating from '@dropins/storefront-pdp/containers/ProductRating.js';

// Inside decorate(): $header and $rating are elements in the block's layout.
await Promise.all([
  pdpRendered.render(ProductHeader, {})($header),
  // Shows the stars and approved review count, or nothing for unrated products.
  pdpRendered.render(ProductRating, {})($rating),
]);
```

To show the average value, for example *4.5 (12)*, pass `valueDisplay: 'value'` and `countDisplay: 'parentheses'`.

<Aside type="tip" title="Show the review list">
`ProductRating` shows only the summary. To show the reviews themselves, add the [Product Reviews block](/merchants/blocks/product-reviews/) from the [Ratings and Reviews drop-in](/dropins/ratings-reviews/).
</Aside>
