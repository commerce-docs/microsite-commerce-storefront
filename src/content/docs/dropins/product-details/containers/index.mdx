Product Details containers display product information, images, pricing, and purchase options:

- **Product Gallery** shows images and videos
- **Product Header**, **Price**, and **Description** present core information
- **Product Options** and **Quantity** handle configuration
- **Gift Card Options** for digital products
- **Product Attributes** display specifications

Customize and position containers independently to create PDP layouts.
