Product Details containers display product information, images, pricing, and purchase options. These modular components enable you to build comprehensive product detail pages (PDPs):

- **Product Gallery** shows images and videos
- **Product Header**, **Price**, and **Description** present core product information
- **Product Options** and **Quantity** handle product configuration
- **Gift Card Options** for digital products
- **Product Attributes** display specifications

Each container can be customized and positioned independently to create your desired PDP layout.
