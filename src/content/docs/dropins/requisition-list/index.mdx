The requisition list drop-in component provides B2B customers with a mechanism to create and manage multiple lists of products for purchasing. Requisition lists enable B2B buyers to organize products, collaborate with team members, and streamline the procurement process.

The requisition list component is fully customizable and can be integrated into your storefront to enhance the B2B shopping experience.

## Use cases

The requisition list drop-in component offers a variety of fully-customizable controls to highlight your products (according to your brand's aesthetics) and create interactive experiences. Use cases include:

- **B2B purchasing**: B2B customers can create multiple requisition lists to organize products for different departments, projects, or purchase orders.
- **Team collaboration**: Multiple team members can add products to shared requisition lists, facilitating collaborative purchasing decisions.
- **Bulk ordering**: Customers can add multiple products to requisition lists and then add them all to cart at once for streamlined checkout.
- **Product organization**: Organize products by project, department, or any other categorization that fits your business needs.
