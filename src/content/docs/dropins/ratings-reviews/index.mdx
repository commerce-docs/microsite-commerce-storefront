The Ratings and Reviews drop-in shows approved shopper reviews on your product pages. Each review shows the reviewer's name, star rating, title, text, and date. Long review lists split into pages. Shoppers can also write reviews, which appear after you approve them.

Average star ratings also appear outside the review list: below the product name on the product details page (PDP), on product listing page (PLP) and search result cards, and on recommendation cards. These ratings come from your catalog, so they load with the product data and need no extra requests.

## What shoppers see

- **Review list**: Approved reviews for the current product, 20 per page by default, in the order Commerce returns them.
- **Page controls**: Previous, next, and page-number buttons when a product has more than one page of reviews.
- **Review form**: A star rating for each rating category, plus **Title**, **Description**, and **Name** fields. After submission, shoppers see a message that the review is pending moderation.
- **Average rating on the product page**: Stars and the approved review count below the product name.
- **Ratings on product cards**: Stars, the average value, and the review count on listing and search cards. Recommendation cards show stars and the review count.

Products without approved reviews show no average rating on product pages or recommendation cards. When product reviews are turned off for the store, the review list and its page controls do not appear.

## How it works

Ratings and reviews come from two data sources. Knowing which source feeds each part of the page helps you find the cause when a rating or review does not appear.

<TableWrapper nowrap={[0]}>

| Storefront element | Data source | Rendered by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review list and page controls | Commerce GraphQL `reviews` query, which returns the approved reviews for one SKU | Ratings and Reviews drop-in: [`Review`](/dropins/ratings-reviews/containers/review/) and [`Pagination`](/dropins/ratings-reviews/containers/pagination/) containers |
| Review settings | Commerce GraphQL `storeConfig` query: `product_reviews_enabled` and `allow_guests_to_write_product_reviews` | Ratings and Reviews drop-in [initializer](/dropins/ratings-reviews/initialization/) |
| Review form | Commerce GraphQL `productReviewRatingsMetadata` query for rating categories and `createProductReview` mutation for submission | Ratings and Reviews drop-in: [`WriteReview`](/dropins/ratings-reviews/containers/write-review/) container |
| Average rating and review count | Catalog Service product attributes `ac_rating_summary` and `ac_review_count` | Product Details [`ProductRating`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-rating/) container, product listing cards, and recommendation cards |

</TableWrapper>

The two catalog attributes carry the review summary for each product:

- **`ac_rating_summary`**: The average rating as a percentage from `0` to `100`. Storefronts divide it by 20 to get a 0–5 star value. For example, `90` displays as 4.5 stars.
- **`ac_review_count`**: The number of approved reviews.

Commerce manages these attributes for you. The SaaS Data Export extension adds them to the product feed and updates the feed when a product's review summary changes. Only approved reviews count toward the summary. Changes reach the storefront after the next product feed synchronization, not immediately. See the <Link href="https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce/saas-data-export/release-notes" text="SaaS Data Export release notes" /> (version 103.6.0).

## Before you begin

Ratings and reviews need setup in three places: the Commerce backend, the catalog attributes, and your storefront code. Complete the backend steps first so that the storefront has data to show.

### Commerce backend

- **Reviews API**: Your Commerce GraphQL endpoint must provide the `reviews` query. This query is part of the [Storefront Compatibility Package](/setup/configuration/storefront-compatibility/). See the <Link href="https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/products/queries/reviews" text="reviews query reference" /> for availability and the full schema.
- **Product reviews turned on**: In the Admin, go to **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **Catalog** > **Catalog** > **Product Reviews** and set **Enabled** to `Yes`.
- **Approved reviews**: Shoppers see only approved reviews. Moderate reviews in **Marketing** > **User Content** > **All Reviews** or **Pending Reviews**. Approved reviews also update the average rating and review count.
- **Rating categories**: Review ratings use the rating categories in **Stores** > **Attributes** > **Rating**. A rating is used only when it is active and assigned to the store view. The review form shows one star row for each of these categories. A review with no rating votes shows *Not rated* in the review list.
- **Guest reviews**: In the same **Product Reviews** settings, set **Allow Guests to Write Reviews**. When it is `No`, signed-out shoppers see a sign-in prompt instead of the review form.
- **reCAPTCHA (optional)**: To protect the review form, enable **Product Review** in the **Google reCAPTCHA Storefront** settings. See [reCAPTCHA](/dropins/ratings-reviews/containers/write-review/#recaptcha).

### Catalog attributes

Catalog Service returns each product attribute with storefront roles, such as `visible_in_pdp` for the product page and `visible_in_plp` for product listings.

- **Product page**: The Product Details drop-in keeps only attributes with the `visible_in_pdp` role. The exporter always turns on this role for both attributes.
- **Product listing and search results**: The product listing setting is off by default for both attributes. If listing and search result cards show no ratings, enable the product listing setting for both attributes on their **Storefront Properties** tabs in **Stores** > **Attributes** > **Product**.

### Storefront

- **Drop-in and block**: Install the drop-in and add the Product Reviews block. See the [Ratings and Reviews quick start](/dropins/ratings-reviews/quick-start/).
- **Review form**: Add a **Write review** button that opens the form. See [Add a Write review button](/dropins/ratings-reviews/quick-start/#add-a-write-review-button).
- **Ratings on other pages**: Add average ratings to the [product page](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-rating/), [product listing cards](/dropins/product-discovery/slots/#show-star-ratings-on-product-cards), and [recommendation cards](/dropins/recommendations/containers/product-list/#show-star-ratings).

<Aside type="note" title="Review text is plain text">
Commerce stores review names, titles, and text exactly as shoppers enter them. The drop-in always renders these fields as escaped text. When you build custom review markup with [slots](/dropins/ratings-reviews/slots/), set shopper text with `textContent`, not `innerHTML`, to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS).
</Aside>
