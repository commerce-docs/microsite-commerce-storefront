Personalization containers deliver targeted content and experiences based on customer segments, behavior, and context. These containers integrate with Adobe Target to show personalized content:

- **Targeted Block** displays personalized content based on targeting rules

Personalization containers can be placed anywhere to deliver customized experiences that increase engagement and conversion.
