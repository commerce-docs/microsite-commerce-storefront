Wishlist containers save and manage favorite products:

- **Wishlist** displays saved products
- **Wishlist Item** shows individual entries
- **Wishlist Toggle** adds and removes products
- **Wishlist Alert** provides notifications

Integrate into product pages, dashboards, and wishlist interfaces.

<TableWrapper nowrap={[0]}>

| Container | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| [Wishlist](/dropins/wishlist/containers/wishlist/) | Complete wishlist with saved products and management. |
| [WishlistItem](/dropins/wishlist/containers/wishlist-item/) | Individual product entries with details and actions. |
| [WishlistToggle](/dropins/wishlist/containers/wishlist-toggle/) | Add or remove products from wishlist. |
| [WishlistAlert](/dropins/wishlist/containers/wishlist-alert/) | Notifications when products are added or removed. |

</TableWrapper>
