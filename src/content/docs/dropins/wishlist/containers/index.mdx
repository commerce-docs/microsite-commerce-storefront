Wishlist containers enable customers to save and manage their favorite products. These containers provide wishlist functionality:

- **Wishlist** displays saved products
- **Wishlist Item** shows individual wishlist entries
- **Wishlist Toggle** adds/removes products
- **Wishlist Alert** provides feedback notifications

Each container can be integrated into product pages, account dashboards, and wishlist management interfaces.
