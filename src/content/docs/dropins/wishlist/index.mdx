The wishlist drop-in component provides both guests and registered customers with a mechanism to store products they are interested in purchasing later.

The wishlist component is fully customizable and can be integrated into your storefront to enhance the shopping experience.

## Use cases

The wishlist drop-in component offers a variety of fully customizable controls to highlight your products (according to your brand's aesthetics) and create interactive experiences. Use cases include:

- **Save for later**: Users can save products they find interesting to a dedicated location for quick access when they are ready to make a purchase.
- **Synchronization**: When a guest user creates an account or logs in, their wishlist items automatically sync across all devices where they are signed in.

## Supported Commerce features

The following table provides an overview of the Adobe Commerce features that the wishlist drop-in component supports:

| Feature          | Status                                       |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Wishlist a product for customers | <Badge text="Supported" variant="tip" />      |
| Move a product from wishlist to cart | <Badge text="Supported" variant="tip" />      |
| Move a product from cart to wishlist | <Badge text="Supported" variant="tip" />      |
| Client-side multistore data isolation | <Badge text="Supported" variant="tip" />      |

<Aside type="note" title="Multistore support">
The wishlist isolates guest wishlist data and cached authenticated data per store view when you pass a `storeCode` during initialization. See [Wishlist multistore support](/dropins/wishlist/multistore/).
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Scope and compatibility">
The drop-in does not provide customer-to-customer wishlist sharing, such as sharing a wishlist by email or link, or multiple named wishlists per customer. Adobe Commerce on Cloud Services (ACCS) behavior for wishlists has changed across releases, so validate end-to-end behavior for your contract and storefront surfaces before you promise parity with legacy Luma experiences.
</Aside>
