The checkout drop-in component provides a variety of fully-customizable controls to help complete a purchase.

These controls include forms to introduce required information for contact details like email address, delivery and billing addresses, shipping options, and payment methods. Established customers who added items to the cart as a guest have the ability to sign in, automatically loading default addresses and contact details.

## Available resources

The checkout drop-in component includes the following resources:

- **[API Functions](/dropins/checkout/functions/)** - Core functions for managing checkout operations like authentication, shipping methods, and order placement
- **[Utility Functions](/dropins/checkout/utilities/)** - Helper functions for DOM manipulation, form handling, data transforms, and more
- **[Containers](/dropins/checkout/containers/)** - Pre-built UI components for checkout steps
- **[Event Handling](/dropins/all/events/)** - Event-driven architecture for component communication

<Aside type="note" title="Configuration and hosting">
Apply gift cards to the order and terms and conditions consent follow your Adobe Commerce checkout configuration. Before you sign off in user acceptance testing, confirm the same rules and extensions are enabled for your hosting model (for example, Adobe Commerce on Cloud Services versus PaaS), because merchants sometimes differ between environments.
