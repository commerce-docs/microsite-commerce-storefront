Checkout containers handle address collection, payment, and order placement:

- **Shipping and Billing** collect addresses
- **Payment Methods** handles payment selection
- **Place Order** completes purchase
- **Login Form** authenticates customers
- Additional containers handle validation, errors, and edge cases

Customize and compose containers to match your checkout workflow.
