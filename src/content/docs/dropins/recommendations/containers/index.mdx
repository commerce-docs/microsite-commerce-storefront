Recommendations containers display AI-powered product suggestions to increase discovery and conversion. These containers integrate with Adobe Sensei to show personalized recommendations:

- **Product List** displays recommended products based on customer behavior and context

Recommendations can be placed on product pages, cart, checkout, or any page to suggest relevant products.
