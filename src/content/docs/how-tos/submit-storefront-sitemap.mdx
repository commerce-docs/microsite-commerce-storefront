Tell Google where to find your storefront's sitemap, then check whether it can read the file. A sitemap lists page URLs for search engines to discover.

## Before you begin

- Your storefront must be published on its production domain.
- You need owner permissions for the correct property in <Link href="https://search.google.com/search-console" text="Google Search Console" />.
- Confirm your sitemap URL with your developer. It is usually `/sitemap.xml` on the production domain. For coverage and additional files, see [Sitemaps](/merchants/edge-delivery-services/sitemaps/).

## Submit the sitemap

<Steps>

1. Open the confirmed production sitemap URL in a browser and check that it returns an XML sitemap without requiring a login.
1. Open Google Search Console and select the property for that production domain.
1. Open **Sitemaps** and enter the confirmed sitemap URL under **Add a new sitemap**. If the field already shows your site's URL prefix, enter the remaining path.
1. Select **Submit**.
1. Open the submitted sitemap's row and check its status. If it is not **Success**, review the reported error with your developer.
1. Repeat the submission for any additional sitemap URLs your developer provided.

</Steps>

## Check the result

The sitemap report should show that Google successfully fetched and processed the file. Check the page-indexing report separately to see which pages have been indexed.

<Aside type="note">
Successful submission does not guarantee that Google will crawl or index every listed URL, or do so within a particular time. Check Search Console periodically for processing and indexing errors.
</Aside>

For permissions, status meanings, and troubleshooting, see <Link href="https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7451001?hl=en" text="Google's Sitemaps report documentation" />.
