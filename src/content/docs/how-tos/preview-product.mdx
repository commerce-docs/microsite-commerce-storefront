Preview a product's images, price, options, and purchasing controls while editing the shared product-page document in Document Authoring.

## Before you begin

- Your content project must contain the `/products/default` document and its `product-details` block.
- Choose an existing product's stock keeping unit (SKU) from your catalog. Find it under **Catalog > Products** in Admin, or ask your developer.

<Aside type="caution">
The `/products/default` document is shared by product pages. Changes to its layout affect all product pages that use this template, not just the product you preview.
</Aside>

## Preview the shared template

<Steps>

1. Open `/products/default` in Document Authoring.
1. Add or update the `defaultSku` row in the `product-details` table with the SKU you chose.

   <TableWrapper>

   | product-details | |
   | --- | --- |
   | defaultSku | HOODIE-001 |

   </TableWrapper>

   Replace `HOODIE-001` with your product's SKU. The first row is the block name; merge its cells in the document.

1. Select the split-screen icon in the right sidebar to open the inline live-preview pane.
1. Confirm that the pane displays the product you chose, including its image, price, and available options.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Use the inline live-preview pane for `/products/default`. Opening that template path in a normal browser tab does not supply a product SKU. The `defaultSku` row is for the editor preview; real product pages normally get their SKU from the URL.
</Aside>

## Check a real product page

<Steps>

1. Open a category or search-results page on your storefront and select the product you want to check.
1. Confirm that the resulting URL follows `/products/<url-key>/<sku>`, such as `/products/cool-hoodie/HOODIE-001`. The URL key and SKU must belong to a real product in your catalog.
1. Confirm that the product page loads the expected catalog data in a normal browser tab.

</Steps>

If the editor preview is empty, check that the `defaultSku` value exists in the catalog available to your storefront. See [Product Details](/merchants/blocks/product-details/) for the block's role and [Add supporting content to product pages](/how-tos/add-supporting-content-to-product-pages/) for changing the shared layout.
