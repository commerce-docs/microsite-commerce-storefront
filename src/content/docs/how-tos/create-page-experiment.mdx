Create an alternate version of a storefront page so your team can compare it with the original in an A/B experiment.

## Before you begin

- Choose the original page and one change to test.
- Ask your developer to confirm that the Experimentation plugin is configured for your storefront, including preview controls, measurement, and any consent requirements.
- Agree with your team on the experiment name, success measure, and launch process.

<Aside type="note">
This procedure prepares and previews page variants. It does not configure the plugin, define your measurement strategy, or launch an experiment automatically. Your team's implementation controls those details.
</Aside>

## Create and connect the variant

<Steps>

1. Duplicate the original page in Document Authoring and give the copy a distinct path, such as `/campaigns/hero-variant`.
1. Make the content change you want to test on the copied page.
1. Preview the variant and confirm that it works on its own.
1. Open the original page and add the experiment properties to its existing metadata table.

   <TableWrapper>

   | metadata | |
   | --- | --- |
   | Experiment | hero-test |
   | Experiment Variants | /campaigns/hero-variant |

   </TableWrapper>

   Replace `hero-test` with your agreed experiment name and `/campaigns/hero-variant` with your variant's path. Merge the first-row cells in the document. For multiple variants, use the comma-separated paths required by your plugin configuration.

   <Diagram caption="Experiment properties in a page's metadata table.">
   ![A metadata table containing an experiment name and paths to variant pages.](@images/MetadataExperiment.png)
   </Diagram>

1. Preview the original page using AEM Sidekick and use your site's experimentation preview controls to compare the original and variant.

</Steps>

## Check the result

Confirm that each version shows its intended content and that shopping controls still work. If you cannot select variants in preview, ask your developer to check the preview integration before continuing.

Follow your team's launch and measurement process before publishing an active experiment. For plugin configuration, see <Link href="https://github.com/adobe/aem-experimentation" text="AEM Experimentation plugin documentation" />. To change labels in a variant, follow [Test alternate storefront labels](/how-tos/test-alternate-storefront-labels/).
