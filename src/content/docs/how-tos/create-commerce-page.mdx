Build a test category page with page metadata, a Commerce block, and section styling, then share a preview with your team. Make the edits in Experience Workspace's **Content** view. Publishing is optional for this exercise.

## Before you begin

Your team must already have a storefront. Ask them for:

- The DA.live link to your storefront's content and access to edit and preview.
- A folder where you can create a test page.
- A category path with products from the connected Commerce catalog.
- A teammate who can review your preview and confirm whether the page should be published.

## How-to: Create a category page

<Tasks>

<Task>

### Create a new document

<Steps>

1. Open the DA.live content link from your team and sign in if prompted.

1. In the content browser, open the folder your team provided for the test page.

1. Select the plus icon at the end of the breadcrumbs to create a document named `dresses`.

1. Open `dresses` in Experience Workspace and select **Content** at the top of the editor.

</Steps>

Use **Content** view for the table edits below. The <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/ew/authoring/editing-docs" text="Experience Workspace editing guide" /> explains the editor's formatting controls.

</Task>

### Add page metadata

**Page metadata** controls page-level settings like the browser tab title, SEO rules, and caching behavior. You configure it using a special table at the top of your document. (See [all page metadata options](/merchants/blocks/page-metadata/) for advanced configuration.)

Create a metadata table at the top of your document:

<tbody>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" style="text-align: center; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">metadata</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">Title</td>
<td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">Dresses</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">Description</td>
<td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">Shop women's dresses in the latest styles, from casual to formal.</td>

</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

These settings give the page a clear title and description for search results, and let it stay cached since a category listing doesn't change on every request.

### Add the Product List Page block

**Commerce blocks** are pre-built components that add shopping functionality to your pages (like product listings, carts, and checkout). You add them using tables where the table name identifies the block and the rows configure its behavior. (See [all commerce blocks](/merchants/blocks/) for the complete list.)

Create a table with the Product List Page block and the category it should display:

<div style="width: 100%; overflow-x: auto;">
<table style="width: 100%; min-width: 470px; max-width: 100%; table-layout: fixed; border-collapse: collapse;">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" style="text-align: center; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">product-list-page</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">urlPath</td>
<td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">/women/dresses</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

This tells the block which category from your catalog to show on this page. Replace `/women/dresses` with a real category path from your own catalog — the block still renders with the placeholder path, but shows zero results if it doesn't match a category you actually have.

</Task>

## Complete page structure

Your document contains three tables in this order:

1. `metadata` table (`Title`, `Description`, `Cache Control`)
1. `product-list-page` block table (`urlPath`)
1. `section-metadata` table (`Style: light`)

## Check your preview

Your first page is ready for review when:

- The preview opens and the browser tab shows the title you entered.
- The expected products appear from the category your team provided.
- The content and layout look correct, and the preview URL is ready to share.

## Publish only when approved

You can finish this exercise with a preview. Publishing makes the page public and requires publishing permissions. Follow your team's approval process before continuing.

<Steps>

1. Select the **Send** icon in Experience Workspace's menu bar.

1. Select **Preview & Publish** from the dropdown.

1. Select **Update** to open the published page in a new browser tab.

</Steps>

If you don't have publishing access, send the approved preview URL to the person your team has authorized to publish. For more about the controls, see <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/ew/authoring/publishing" text="Publishing content" />.

## Troubleshooting

<TableWrapper>

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Can't open the site or document | Ask the teammate who provided your DA.live link to confirm the location and your editing access. |
| Can't find Content view | Open the document in Experience Workspace and select **Content** at the top of the editor. If your project uses a different editor, check the [visual editing overview](/merchants/quick-start/choose-visual-editor/) with your team. |
| Block not appearing | Check that the block name is `product-list-page` (kebab-case). |
| Page shows no products | Check that `urlPath` matches the category path your team provided. Ask the teammate who supplied it to confirm that the category contains products. |
| Changes not visible | Repeat [Preview your page](#preview-your-page), then reload the preview tab. If needed, hard refresh with Cmd+Shift+R on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows. |
| Can't publish | Ask your team's authorized publisher to publish the approved preview. Publishing is not required to complete this exercise. |

</TableWrapper>
