Add a campaign banner, carousel, headings, or other supporting content above or below the shopping controls on your product pages.

## Before you begin

Open `/products/default` in Document Authoring and choose a content block supported by your storefront. Use [Preview a product](/how-tos/preview-product/) to select a real product for the inline preview.

<Aside type="caution">
This document is shared by product pages. Content you add here appears on all pages that use this template. To show content only for selected products or categories, use [Show enrichment content for selected products or categories](/how-tos/show-enrichment-content/) instead.
</Aside>

## Add the supporting content

<Steps>

1. Find the `product-details` table in `/products/default`.
1. Place your content above or below that table, in the order shoppers should see it. You can insert ordinary text or images, or [add a supported block table](/how-tos/add-block-to-page/).

   For example, insert a carousel table above the product details:

   <TableWrapper>

   | carousel | |
   | --- | --- |
   | Your first campaign image | First slide caption |
   | Your second campaign image | Second slide caption |

   </TableWrapper>

   Merge the first-row cells in the document. Replace the image labels with actual images, and replace the captions with your text. Each row below the header is one slide.

1. Open the inline live-preview pane and confirm that the content appears in the intended position relative to the product details.
1. Preview another product to check that the content is appropriate for the shared template.
1. Publish the updated document when the layout looks right.

</Steps>

## Check the result

Open a real product URL on the published storefront. Confirm that the supporting content appears in the expected order and that the product's shopping controls remain available.

For recommendation units, follow [Add product recommendations to a page](/how-tos/add-product-recommendations/).
