title: Feature a product on a campaign page
description: Display a product on a campaign page using SKU metadata, with automatic block placement or a custom position.
---

import { Aside, Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';
import TableWrapper from '@components/TableWrapper.astro';

Display one product's shopping controls directly on a campaign page by setting the product's SKU in page metadata. Your storefront adds the Product Details block as the first item on the page, so you do not need to add a block table.

## Before you begin

Choose an existing product from the catalog available to your storefront. Find its SKU under **Catalog > Products** in Admin, or ask your developer. Your storefront must already support the Product Details block.

<Aside type="note">
Prefer linking to the product's existing page when that meets your campaign's needs. Use this procedure when shoppers need the product's purchasing controls on the campaign page itself.
</Aside>

## Add the product

<Steps>

1. Create or open your campaign document in Document Authoring, for example, `/campaigns/new-arrivals`.
1. Find the page's metadata table and add a `sku` row. If there is no metadata table, follow [Add page metadata](/how-tos/add-page-metadata/) to create one.

   <TableWrapper>

   | metadata | |
   | --- | --- |
   | sku | HOODIE-001 |

   </TableWrapper>

   Replace `HOODIE-001` with your product's SKU. Merge the cells in the first row of the metadata table in your document, and retain its other properties.

1. Select the paper-plane button, then **Preview**, to open the campaign page.
1. Check the product's image, name, price, and options. Publish the page when the result looks right.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Place the product below other content">
To show the product below a banner or other content, insert a `product-details` table at that position in your document. A header-only table with no settings rows is enough. The `sku` metadata still identifies the product to display.
</Aside>

## Check the result

Open the published campaign page and confirm that it displays the selected product with purchasing controls. If the wrong product appears, check the `sku` metadata value against your catalog.

For changes to the product page shared by your catalog, use [Add supporting content to product pages](/how-tos/add-supporting-content-to-product-pages/) instead.
