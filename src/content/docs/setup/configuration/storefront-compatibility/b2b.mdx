On Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service and Adobe Commerce Optimizer, Adobe installs and updates the B2B Storefront Compatibility Package automatically. This page describes the GraphQL schema extensions the package adds for B2B storefront drop-ins.

<Aside type="note" title="Version and compatibility requirements">
Adobe Commerce B2B 1.5.2 only. Magento Open Source is not supported.

On Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service and Adobe Commerce Optimizer, Adobe installs and updates this package automatically. You do not install it with Composer on Commerce PaaS or on-premises. B2B drop-in components also require the B2C [Storefront Compatibility Package](/setup/configuration/storefront-compatibility/).
</Aside>

## Major features

The `1.0.22` package line adds the following GraphQL queries, mutations, and schema extensions:

#### Company hierarchy

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/company/mutations/assign-child-company/"} text={"assignChildCompany"} /> mutation — Company administrators can assign a child company to a parent company within the company hierarchy.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/company/mutations/unassign-child-company/"} text={"unassignChildCompany"} /> mutation — Company administrators can unassign a child company from its parent company within the company hierarchy.

- `company_hierarchy` on the `Customer` type — Company relation hierarchies for companies the customer administers.

- `is_admin` on `CompanyBasicInfo` — Indicates whether the company is the admin company in a returned hierarchy.

#### Company configuration and roles

- `company_enabled`, `allow_company_registration`, and `company_credit_enabled` on `StoreConfig` — B2B company, registration, and credit settings exposed to the storefront.

- `status` on `Company` and `CompanyBasicInfo` — Current company approval status (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `BLOCKED`).

- `roles` on `Company` — Paginated list of company roles.

- `available_payment_methods` on `Company` — Payment methods available to the company with B2B-specific filtering.

- `available_shipping_methods` on `Company` — Shipping carriers available to the company with B2B-specific filtering.

- `credit` on `Company` — Company credit data, including `exceed_limit` on the `CompanyCredit` type to indicate whether orders can exceed the credit limit.

- `id` on the `Customer` type — Customer ID for B2B company management GraphQL operations.

#### Requisition lists

- `requisition_lists` on the `Customer` type — Paginated, filterable, and sortable requisition lists for the signed-in customer.

- `items` on `RequisitionList` — Paginated line items on a requisition list.

- `sku` on `RequisitionListItemInterface` — Product SKU on a requisition list line item.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/requisition-list/queries/shared-requisition-list/"} text={"sharedRequisitionList"} /> query — Retrieves a read-only shared requisition list for B2B customers, including sender details and associated items, based on the provided token.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/requisition-list/mutations/share-requisition-list-by-token/"} text={"shareRequisitionListByToken"} /> mutation — Generates a shareable storefront token that enables B2B customers to share a requisition list with colleagues within the same company.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/requisition-list/mutations/share-requisition-list-by-email/"} text={"shareRequisitionListByEmail"} /> mutation — Sends a requisition list to colleagues within the same company via email for B2B customers.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/requisition-list/mutations/import-shared-requisition-list/"} text={"importSharedRequisitionList"} /> mutation — Imports a shared requisition list into the current customer account based on the provided token.

- `requisition_list_sharing_enabled`, `requisition_list_share_link_validity_days`, `requisition_list_share_max_recipients`, and `requisition_list_share_storefront_path` on `StoreConfig` — Requisition list sharing settings for the storefront.

#### Negotiable quotes

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/negotiable-quote/queries/templates/"} text={"negotiableQuoteTemplates"} /> query — Retrieves negotiable quote templates with sales rep, order, and pricing details.

- `historyV2` on `NegotiableQuoteTemplate` — Template change history, including expiration date updates.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/negotiable-quote/mutations/set-quote-template-expiration-date/"} text={"setQuoteTemplateExpirationDate"} /> mutation — Sets an expiration date on negotiable quote templates.

- <Link href={"https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/webapi/graphql/schema/b2b/negotiable-quote/mutations/place-order-v2/"} text={"placeNegotiableQuoteOrderV2"} /> mutation — Transforms a negotiable quote into an order and returns full order details, including an errors array for standardized response handling.

- `expiration_date`, `sales_rep_name`, `template_id`, `template_name`, and `history` on `NegotiableQuote` — Template linkage and quote history on negotiable quotes.

- `quote_enabled` on the `Customer` type — Indicates whether negotiable quote functionality is enabled for the customer.

- `quote_minimum_amount` and `quote_minimum_amount_message` on `StoreConfig` — Minimum order amount required to request a quote.

#### Purchase orders

- `purchase_orders_enabled` on the `Customer` type — Indicates whether purchase order functionality is enabled for the customer.

- `approvePurchaseOrders` mutation — Approves one or more purchase orders pending the customer's action.

- `approval_flow` on `PurchaseOrder` — Approval steps for a purchase order.

- `my_approvals` on `PurchaseOrdersFilterInput` — Filters purchase orders pending the customer's approval or already handled by the customer.
