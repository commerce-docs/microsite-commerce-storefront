Document Authoring (DA.live) manages the page documents for your Adobe Commerce storefront. Use it to organize content, collaborate on changes, and manage translations and publishing separately from the storefront code.

Open those documents in Experience Workspace to edit their content, then preview changes before publishing through Edge Delivery Services.

## How to use the Document Authoring tool

Ask your team for the DA.live link to your storefront's content and access to edit and preview pages. Then choose a task:

- [Create your first commerce page](/how-tos/create-commerce-page/): Build a test category page and preview the result.
- [Translate storefront content](/how-tos/translate-storefront-content/): Translate page documents and coordinate Commerce checks with your developer.
- <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/ew/authoring" text="Authoring with Experience Workspace" />: Edit documents, manage versions, and publish content.

## In-context editing

To edit content while viewing the storefront page, use [Experience Workspace](/merchants/quick-start/experience-workspace/). Follow its setup instructions if your project has not enabled visual editing yet.
