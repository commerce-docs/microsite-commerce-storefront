The Universal Editor lets you edit storefront content directly on the rendered page in DA.live — click any element and change it in context. It is available for all Commerce backend types: PaaS, Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service, and Adobe Commerce Optimizer. **Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service and Adobe Commerce Optimizer customers use the same setup, configuration, and workflow as PaaS** — no additional UE steps. The editing workflow is identical regardless of backend.

<Aside type="note" title="Prerequisite">
Your storefront must already be set up and your content initialized in DA.live before you can use the Universal Editor. See [Create a storefront](/get-started/create-storefront/) if you have not completed setup.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Open your storefront page in [DA.live](https://da.live).
2. Select the **Open in-context editing** button from the toolbar.
3. Click any element on the rendered page to edit text, images, or blocks in context.
4. Use **Sidekick** to preview and publish your changes.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Don't see the button?">
If **Open in-context editing** is not visible in the toolbar, your site may need Universal Editor enabled first. Ask your developer or see [Universal Editor for developers](/boilerplate/universal-editor/).
</Aside>

## Known issues and limitations

- **Early Access**: Universal Editor for DA.live is part of Adobe's Early Access Technology program and requires AEM Sites credits on your IMS Org. Contact your Adobe account team to confirm eligibility.
- **Browser support**: Only Chrome and Safari are supported. Firefox and other browsers are not supported at this time.
- **Content fragments**: Editing content fragments with Universal Editor on DA.live (Author Bus) is not supported.
