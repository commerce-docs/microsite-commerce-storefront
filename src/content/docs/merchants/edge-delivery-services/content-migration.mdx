You can copy all pages from one <Term>Document Authoring</Term> project on <Link href="https://da.live" text="DA.live" /> to another using three apps: Traverse, Import, and Bulk Operations. Use this workflow to migrate content from one project to another, such as when you move tested content to your main project or create a copy of a project for a new environment.

   <Diagram caption="Migrate content between Document Authoring projects.">
    ![Migrate content between Document Authoring projects.](@images/merchants/MigrationProcess.svg
    )
   </Diagram>

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Write access to your source and target DA.live project folders
- Your organization name and the target project name on DA.live
- Access to your target project's code repository on GitHub

## Migrate your content

Go to <Link href="https://da.live" text="DA.live" /> to begin. You'll copy all pages from your source project to your target project and publish them.

<Tasks>

<Task>
### Traverse your source project.

In the <Link href="https://da.live/apps" text="Apps" /> section, open the Traverse app. Enter your source project's `/org/name` and click **Crawl**. The app returns a list of file paths for every page in your project. Copy that list.

<Diagram caption="Traverse your source project.">
    ![Traverse your source project.](@images/merchants/TraverseSourceProject.png)
</Diagram>

</Task>

<Task>

### Import your content into the target project.

In the Apps section, open the Import app. Paste your source project's file paths into the **By Url** textbox. Enter your organization name and site name in the **Into** fields. Click **Import** to copy the files into the target project.

<Diagram caption="Import your content into the target project.">
    ![Import your content into the target project.](@images/merchants/ImportContent.png)
</Diagram>

</Task>

<Task>

### Traverse your target project.

Open the Traverse app again. Enter your target project's `/org/name` and click **Crawl**. Copy the list of target project file paths. You'll paste them into the Bulk Operations app in the next step.

<Diagram caption="Traverse your target project.">
    ![Traverse your target project.](@images/merchants/TraverseTargetProject.png)
</Diagram>

</Task>

<Task>

### Preview and publish your target project files.

Open the Bulk Operations app from the Apps section. Enter the file paths from the previous step into the **URLs** textbox. Select **Preview** from the dropdown and click **Submit** to make the pages available on your preview URL. Then select **Publish** from the dropdown and click **Submit** to publish the pages.

<Diagram caption="Preview and publish your target project files.">
    ![Preview and publish your target project files.](@images/merchants/PreviewAndPublish.png)
</Diagram>

</Task>

</Tasks>

## Verify the migration.

   Open your target project on DA.live, select a few migrated pages, and open them in preview to confirm the content is correct.
