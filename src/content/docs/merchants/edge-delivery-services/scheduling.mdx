## Overview

Scheduled publishing lets you prepare content in advance and control exactly when it goes live. This is useful for:

- **Product launches**: Coordinate content updates across multiple pages to go live at a specific time.
- **Promotions**: Schedule sale pages and banners to publish when your campaign starts.
- **Coordinated releases**: Ensure related pages (landing page, product pages, FAQs) all appear together.

## How it works

1. **Register your site**: A one-time setup that enables scheduling for your organization and site.
2. **Create content**: Author your pages as usual in Document Authoring (da.live) or your preferred tool.
3. **Schedule publishing**: Select the pages you want to publish together and set a future date and time.
4. **Automatic publishing**: Your content goes live at the scheduled time—no manual intervention needed.

## Before you begin

Your administrator needs to register your organization and site for scheduled publishing. This requires:

- Your organization and site names
- An AEM Admin API token
- An Admin API key for the scheduler service

Once registered, authors can schedule content for publishing.
