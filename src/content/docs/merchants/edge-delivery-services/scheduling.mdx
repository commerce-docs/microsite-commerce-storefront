Schedule content to publish at a future time. Create snapshots to group multiple pages together so they all go live simultaneously.

## Overview

Scheduled publishing lets you prepare content in advance and control when it goes live:

- **Product launches**: Coordinate content updates across multiple pages to go live at a specific time.
- **Promotions**: Schedule sale pages and banners to publish when your campaign starts.
- **Coordinated releases**: Publish related pages (landing page, product pages, FAQs) together.

## How it works

<Steps>
1. **Create content**. Author your pages in <Link href="/merchants/quick-start/document-authoring/" text="Document Authoring (da.live)" />.
1. **Create a snapshot**. Add the pages you want to publish together to a snapshot. Snapshots let you group content for a launch or event and review it before publishing. See the <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/snapshots-reviews" text="Snapshots and Reviews" /> guide on aem.live.
1. **Schedule publishing**. Configure when the snapshot will go live using a crontab in the `.helix` folder of your content source (Document Authoring). Your content goes live at the scheduled time with no manual intervention.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Step-by-step instructions">
For detailed instructions, see the <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/scheduling" text="Scheduled publishing" /> guide on aem.live. For recurring tasks (for example, publishing an index on a schedule), see <Link href="https://www.aem.live/docs/recurring" text="Schedule recurring tasks" />.
</Aside>
