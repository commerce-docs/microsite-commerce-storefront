Company administrators view and reorganize parent and child companies through this block.

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

### Important notes

- Only users with the Company Administrator role can see the hierarchy. Other users see the message *You do not have permission to view company hierarchy.*
- The hierarchy has one level: parent companies and their direct child companies. Company administrators can move a child company to a different parent company.
- The block does not add a page heading. Add a heading above the block if your page needs one.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company/hierarchy` page. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). For details about the hierarchy, see the [CompanyHierarchy container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-hierarchy/).
