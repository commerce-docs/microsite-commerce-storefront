Buyers view and manage their quotes, send them for review, and complete the checkout process through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See a list of their quotes with each quote's status, and page through it.
- Open a quote to see its details, items and prices, shipping information, and comments.
- Print, copy, rename, close, or delete a quote, create a quote template from it, attach files, and send it for review.
- Complete the checkout process for the quote when it is ready for checkout. Only the buyer who owns the quote can complete the checkout process.

The block shows the list when the page opens without a quote ID, and one quote when the URL includes a `quoteid` parameter. For details, see the [ManageNegotiableQuote container](/dropins-b2b/quote-management/containers/manage-negotiable-quote/) and the [QuotesListTable container](/dropins-b2b/quote-management/containers/quotes-list-table/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features and negotiable quotes in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company** and **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable B2B Quote**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/negotiable-quote` page. Use this exact path, because other blocks link to it. When a buyer checks out a quote, this block opens the `/b2b/quote-checkout` page. For the page titles and metadata, see [Quote pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#quotes).

## Who can see what

The quote actions a buyer sees depend on the **Quotes** permissions in their company role. **Request, Edit, Delete** controls editing, sending, and deleting a quote. **Checkout with quote** controls checkout.

## Labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `quote-management` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The exceptions are:

- The **Checkout** button on a quote uses the `Cart.PriceSummary.checkout` key in the `cart` sheet.
- The **Access Restricted** message, which buyers see when company features are turned off or they do not belong to a company, is set in the block code.
- The **View** button, **No Quotes Found** message, **Items Quoted**, **Comments**, **History Log** tab names, and **Select** placeholder are set in the drop-in code.

You cannot change text that is set in code with a sheet.
