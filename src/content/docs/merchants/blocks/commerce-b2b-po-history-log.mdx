Buyers see when each action on a purchase order happened, such as when it was submitted, approved, or rejected, through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See each action on the purchase order, such as when it was submitted, approved, rejected, or placed as an order. The block shows five entries at first. When there are more, buyers select **View More** to see the rest.

For details, see the [PurchaseOrderHistoryLog container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/purchase-order-history-log/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**, and then for each company in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. In your storefront configuration, set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. If it's missing or `false`, the storefront sends customers to their account page. Customers need the **View my Purchase Orders** permission in their company role. Without it, the storefront sends them to their account page. Company Administrators don't need this permission.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/purchase-order-details` page, together with the other purchase order detail blocks. Use this exact path, because other blocks link to it. For the block order, layout, and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
