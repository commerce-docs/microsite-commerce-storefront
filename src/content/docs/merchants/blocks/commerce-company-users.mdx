Company users see their company's user accounts, and users with permission add, edit, and remove them using this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the list of company users, show all, active, or inactive users, and page through the list.
- Add, edit, and remove users, and set a user to Active or Inactive.

For details, see the [CompanyUsers container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-users/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Customers need the **View users and teams** permission in their company role to see users, and **Manage users and teams** to change them. Company Administrators have both.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company/users` page. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
