Buyers see the purchase order confirmation and purchase order number through this block. The Checkout and Quote Checkout blocks show it after a buyer places a purchase order.

## What buyers can do

- See that their purchase order was submitted, with its purchase order number.
- Open the purchase order's details.

For details, see the [PurchaseOrderConfirmation container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/purchase-order-confirmation/).

## Requirements

Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**, and then for each company in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. For the Checkout block to place purchase orders, set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true` in your storefront configuration. If it's missing or `false`, the Checkout block places a regular order.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

You do not need to add this block to a page. The [Checkout](/merchants/blocks/commerce-checkout/) and [Quote Checkout](/merchants/blocks/commerce-b2b-quote-checkout/) blocks show it in place of the checkout form after a buyer places a purchase order. It links to the purchase order on the `/customer/purchase-order-details` page. See [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Continue shopping** button, which opens the home page, is set in the block code, so you cannot change it in a sheet.
