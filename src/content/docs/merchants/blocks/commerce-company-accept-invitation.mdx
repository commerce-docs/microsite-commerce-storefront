Invited users join companies through this block.

## What buyers can do

- Open the invitation link from their email and join the company.
- See whether the invitation was accepted, or a message when the link is invalid or expired or when company features are turned off.
- Go to their account with the **My Account** button when they are signed in. Customers who are not signed in see a **Go to Login** button instead, unless company features are turned off.

For details, see the [AcceptInvitation container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/accept-invitation/).

## Requirements

Customers do not need to sign in to use this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the page that company invitation emails link to. No storefront code sets this path, so confirm with your developer which path your invitation emails use. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
