Buyers see the purchase orders created by the company users who report to them through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the purchase orders created by the company users who report to them, 10, 20, or 30 per page (10 by default).
- Open a purchase order's details.
- Company Administrators can also select several purchase orders and approve or reject them together. They can select only purchase orders that are pending approval or that require approval.

For details, see the [CompanyPurchaseOrders container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/company-purchase-orders/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**, and then for each company in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. In your storefront configuration, set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. If it's missing or `false`, the storefront sends customers to their account page. Customers need the **Order Approvals** permission in their company role, or the storefront sends them to their account page. The block appears only for customers who also have **View for all company**. Company Administrators don't need these permissions.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/purchase-orders` page, together with the other purchase order list blocks. Use this exact path, because other blocks link to it. For the block order, layout, and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Selected** count and the **Approve selected** and **Reject selected** buttons that Company Administrators see are set in the drop-in code, so you cannot change them in a sheet.
