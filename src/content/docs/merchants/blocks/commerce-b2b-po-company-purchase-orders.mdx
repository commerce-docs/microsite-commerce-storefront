Add this block to the purchase orders page so buyers can review purchase orders from company users who report to them. The block has no settings to fill in.

## Page setup

Place this block alongside the personal purchase order list and the approval list so buyers can find company orders on the same page.

<Steps>

1. Open the document for `/customer/purchase-orders` in your authoring tool.
1. Add a one-cell block table after Purchase Orders Requiring Approval.

</Steps>

Use this block name in the table's only cell:

<TableWrapper>

| commerce-b2b-po-company-purchase-orders |
| --- |

</TableWrapper>

Use this exact page path, because other blocks link to it. For the layout and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Configuration

Leave the block table with just the block name. This block has no settings; manage access through the requirements below.

## Requirements

Ask your Commerce administrator or developer to confirm these settings before you preview the page.

<TableWrapper nowrap={[0]}>

| Where | What to set up |
| ----- | -------------- |
| Commerce Admin | Turn on company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. |
| Commerce Admin | Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. |
| Commerce Admin | For each company, turn on purchase orders in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. |
| Storefront configuration | Set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. Developers manage this setting in `config.json` or the Config Service. |
| Company administrator's storefront account | In **Roles and Permissions**, give the **Order Approvals** and **View for all company** permissions to the roles of customers who use this block. The company administrator has full permissions by default. |

</TableWrapper>

## Check the page

Preview the page before publishing to check that the block is available to the intended buyers.

<Steps>

1. Preview `/customer/purchase-orders`.
1. Sign in as the company administrator or as a customer whose role has both permissions listed above, in a company with purchase orders turned on.
1. Check that the company purchase order list appears.

</Steps>

If the storefront sends you to the sign-in page, sign in before checking the block. If it sends you to the account page, ask your developer to check `commerce-b2b-enabled` and your company administrator to check **Order Approvals**. If the page opens but this block is missing, ask your company administrator to check **View for all company**. Company administrators do not need these role permissions.

## What buyers can do

- See the purchase orders created by the company users who report to them, 10, 20, or 30 per page (10 by default).
- Open a purchase order's details.
- Company Administrators can also select several purchase orders and approve or reject them together. They can select only purchase orders that are pending approval or that require approval.

For details, see the [CompanyPurchaseOrders container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/company-purchase-orders/).

## Labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Selected** count and the **Approve selected** and **Reject selected** buttons that Company Administrators see are set in the drop-in code, so you cannot change them in a sheet.
