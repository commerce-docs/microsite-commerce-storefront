Add this block to the purchase orders page so buyers can review, approve, or reject purchase orders that need their approval.

## What buyers can do

- See the purchase orders that are waiting for their approval, 10, 20, or 30 per page (10 by default). The list also includes purchase orders they could approve that have already been handled.
- Select several purchase orders and approve or reject them together. Buyers can select only purchase orders that are pending approval or that require approval.
- Open a purchase order's details.

For details, see the [RequireApprovalPurchaseOrders container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/require-approval-purchase-orders/).

## Before you begin

Ask your Commerce administrator or developer to confirm these settings before you add the block.

<TableWrapper nowrap={[0]}>

| Where | What to set up |
| ----- | -------------- |
| Commerce Admin | Turn on company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. |
| Commerce Admin | Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. |
| Commerce Admin | For each company, turn on purchase orders in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. |
| Storefront configuration | Set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. Developers manage this setting in `config.json` or the Config Service. |
| Company administrator's storefront account | In **Roles and Permissions**, give the **Order Approvals** permission to the roles of customers who use this block. The company administrator has full permissions by default. |

</TableWrapper>

## Add the block

Place the block alongside the other purchase order lists so buyers can find their approval tasks on the same page.

<Steps>

1. Open the document for `/customer/purchase-orders` in your authoring tool.
1. Add a one-cell block table after Customer Purchase Orders and before Company Purchase Orders.

</Steps>

This block has no settings. Use this block name in the table's only cell:

<div style="width: 100%; overflow-x: auto;">
<table style="width: 100%; min-width: 470px; max-width: 100%; table-layout: fixed; border-collapse: collapse;">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: center; padding: 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">commerce-b2b-po-require-approval-purchase-orders</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

Use this exact page path, because other blocks link to it. For the layout and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Check the page

Preview the page before publishing to check that the block is available to the intended buyers.

<Steps>

1. Preview `/customer/purchase-orders`.
1. Sign in as the company administrator or as a user whose role has **Order Approvals**, in a company with purchase orders turned on.
1. Check that the purchase order list appears.

</Steps>

Customers must sign in to see this block. The storefront sends guests to the sign-in page. It sends signed-in customers to their account page when:

- `commerce-b2b-enabled` is missing or `false`.
- Their company role doesn't have the **Order Approvals** permission.
- Purchase orders are turned off for the store or for their company.

The last two cases don't apply to the company administrator.

## Change labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Selected** count and the **Approve selected** and **Reject selected** buttons are set in the drop-in code, so you cannot change them in a sheet.
