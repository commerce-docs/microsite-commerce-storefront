Buyers approve or reject the purchase orders that need their approval through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the purchase orders that are waiting for their approval, 10, 20, or 30 per page (10 by default). The list also includes purchase orders they could approve that have already been handled.
- Select several purchase orders and approve or reject them together. Buyers can select only purchase orders that are pending approval or that require approval.
- Open a purchase order's details.

For details, see the [RequireApprovalPurchaseOrders container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/require-approval-purchase-orders/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**, and then for each company in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. In your storefront configuration, set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. If it's missing or `false`, the storefront sends customers to their account page. Customers need the **Order Approvals** permission in their company role, or the storefront sends them to their account page. Company Administrators don't need this permission.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/purchase-orders` page, together with the other purchase order list blocks. Use this exact path, because other blocks link to it. For the block order, layout, and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Selected** count and the **Approve selected** and **Reject selected** buttons are set in the drop-in code, so you cannot change them in a sheet.
