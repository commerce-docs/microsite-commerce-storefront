Company users see their company's teams and users, and users with permission organize them, through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the company's teams and users as a tree.
- Add, edit, move, and remove users and teams.
- See a **Create a Company Account** link when they do not belong to a company yet. The link opens `/customer/company/create`.

For details, see the [CompanyStructure container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-structure/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Customers need the **View users and teams** permission in their company role to see the structure, and **Manage users and teams** to change it. Company Administrators have both. The block also sends every signed-in customer to their account page when **Allow Company Registration from the Storefront** is turned off in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **Customers** > **Company Configuration** > **General**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company/structure` page. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Save** and **Cancel** buttons in the team form are set in the drop-in code, so you cannot change them in a sheet.
