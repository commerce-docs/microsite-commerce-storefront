Company users with permission view and manage the company's roles, and the permissions each role includes, through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the company's roles.
- Add, edit, duplicate, and delete roles, and choose the permissions each role includes.

For details, see the [RolesAndPermissions container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/roles-and-permissions/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Customers need **View roles and permissions** or **Manage roles and permissions** in their company role to see roles. Only **Manage roles and permissions** lets them change roles. Company Administrators have both.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company/roles` page. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
