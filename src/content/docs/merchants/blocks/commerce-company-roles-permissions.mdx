Company administrators control who can access B2B features (such as company credit, negotiable quotes, purchase orders, and requisition lists) through this block. Place it on roles and permissions pages.

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.
