The Product Details block renders the page that shoppers see when they view a single product: the image gallery, price, options, add-to-cart button, and more. Most shopper sessions go through the product page before a purchase, so it is one of the most important Commerce blocks on your site. Your storefront already includes this block, so you do not need to add it from scratch.

## What shoppers see

Product names, prices, images, and descriptions all come from your catalog, so you do not create or edit them here. Code controls the layout and what appears in each region, not the block table in Document Authoring.

## Preview a product

This is the task you'll use most often. The document at `/products/default` is a template for every product page on your storefront, so changes you make there apply to all product URLs. Sample pages already include a `defaultSku` row. Set it to a SKU in your catalog and open the inline live-preview pane to see real product data while you edit.

<Aside type="tip" title="Confirm the document at /products/default exists">
Open `/products/default` in your content folder in Document Authoring. If the document is missing or empty, you cannot preview the product page.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. In <Term>Document Authoring</Term>, open the document at `/products/default`.

1. Add or update the `product-details` table with a `defaultSku` row that points to a SKU in your catalog.

   To find a SKU, go to **Catalog → Products** in Admin, or ask your integrator.

   <table style="width: 100%; min-width: 470px; max-width: 100%; table-layout: fixed; border-collapse: collapse;">
   <tbody>
   <tr>
   <td colspan="2" style="text-align: center; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">product-details</td>
   </tr>
   <tr>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">defaultSku</td>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">HOODIE-001</td>
   </tr>
   </tbody>
   </table>

1. Click the split-screen icon on the right sidebar to open the inline live-preview pane.

   The PDP renders using the SKU in the `defaultSku` row.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Use the inline live-preview pane, not the paper-plane button">
The paper-plane (Publish or Preview) button does not work at `/products/default`. It opens the page without a product SKU, so the block redirects to `/notfound`. This is expected for a template path. Use the inline live-preview pane instead, which reads the `defaultSku` row from the block table.
</Aside>

The `defaultSku` row applies only at `/products/default` in the inline live-preview pane. On real product URLs, the SKU comes from the URL, so you do not need to keep `defaultSku` in sync with catalog changes.

</TaskSection>

## View a real product page

To confirm that a real product page renders (not just the preview at `/products/default`):

<Steps>

1. Find a product's URL key and SKU in **Catalog → Products** in Admin, or open a category or search results page on your storefront and click any product.

1. Open the product URL, which follows the pattern `/products/<url-key>/<sku>` (for example, `/products/cool-hoodie/HOODIE-001`).

1. Confirm the product page loads with live catalog data.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
A real product URL works in a normal browser tab and through the Publish to Preview button, because the SKU comes from the URL. The `/products/default` document is the exception: it needs the inline live-preview pane instead, as described in [Preview a product](/merchants/blocks/product-details/#preview-a-product).
</Aside>

</TaskSection>

## Feature a product on a marketing page

To highlight one product on a custom page (for example, a landing page for a campaign):

<Steps>

1. Create your marketing page in Document Authoring at the path you want, for example, `/campaigns/new-arrivals`.

1. Add the `product-details` block to the page. The block renders the product UI (gallery, price, options, and so on). You do not need any rows inside the block table. A header-only table is enough:

   <table style="width: 100%; min-width: 470px; max-width: 100%; table-layout: fixed; border-collapse: collapse;">
   <tbody>
   <tr>
   <td style="text-align: center; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">product-details</td>
   </tr>
   </tbody>
   </table>

1. Add a `sku` row to your metadata table, or create one as shown here:

   <table style="width: 100%; min-width: 470px; max-width: 100%; table-layout: fixed; border-collapse: collapse;">
   <tbody>
   <tr>
   <td colspan="2" style="text-align: center; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">metadata</td>
   </tr>
   <tr>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">sku</td>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">HOODIE-001</td>
   </tr>
   </tbody>
   </table>

   If your page already has a metadata table, add a new `sku` row to it. You do not need a second table. For page metadata and its other properties, see [Page metadata](/merchants/blocks/page-metadata/).

1. Click the paper plane button, then click the Preview button to preview the page

   The block reads the `sku` from page metadata and renders that product in a new tab.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">

Use this pattern sparingly. Every product already has its own page at `/products/<url-key>/<sku>`. Adding a second hand-built page for the same product means you have to keep both in sync when the catalog changes. Prefer linking from your campaign page to the product's existing URL whenever you can.

</Aside>

</TaskSection>

## Add companion blocks to a product page

Your storefront comes with several blocks already set up at `/products/default`. Each block is a separate table in the document, and the page renders them in the order they appear. That means you can add a banner, a carousel, or a [Product Recommendations](/merchants/content-customizations/product-recommendations/) block by inserting a new table at the right spot.

<Steps>

1. In Document Authoring, open the document at `/products/default`.

1. Click above the `product-details` table and insert a new table for the carousel. Each row below the header is one slide. Add your campaign image in the left cell and a caption in the right:

   <table style="width: 100%; min-width: 470px; max-width: 100%; table-layout: fixed; border-collapse: collapse;">
   <tbody>
   <tr>
   <td colspan="2" style="text-align: center; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); background-color: var(--sl-color-gray-6); font-weight: 600;">carousel</td>
   </tr>
   <tr>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); color: var(--sl-color-gray-3); font-style: italic;">image</td>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">Slide 1</td>
   </tr>
   <tr>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5); color: var(--sl-color-gray-3); font-style: italic;">image</td>
   <td style="width: 50%; padding: 0.4rem 0.75rem; border: 1px solid var(--sl-color-gray-5);">Slide 2</td>
   </tr>
   </tbody>
   </table>

1. Open the inline live-preview pane to confirm the carousel appears above the product details.

</Steps>

When you save, the carousel table sits above your `product-details` table. Shoppers see the carousel first, then scroll down to the product details. The same principle applies to any other companion block: add it where you want it to appear, and the page renders it there.

</TaskSection>
