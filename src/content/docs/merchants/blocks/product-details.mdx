The `product-details` block renders the product detail page (PDP) for your storefront. Most shopper sessions go through a PDP before a purchase, so it is one of the most important Commerce blocks on your site.

If you are new to Commerce blocks, read [Authoring Commerce blocks](/merchants/quick-start/authoring-commerce-blocks/) first. It explains what you can change without code and what to hand to a developer. The rest of this page covers what is specific to the PDP.

## Where this block lives in your boilerplate

You do not add the `product-details` block from scratch. The Adobe Commerce boilerplate ships with PDP wiring already in place:

- The `product-details` block is authored on a single document at `/products/default` — sometimes called the PDP template — so you can preview and style a PDP in <Term>Document Authoring</Term> without a real product URL.
- Real shopper URLs follow the pattern `/products/<url-key>/<sku>` (for example, `/products/cool-hoodie/HOODIE-001`). The boilerplate reads the SKU from the URL and renders the same `product-details` block for every product.

<Aside type="note" title="Product content comes from Commerce, not from this document">
Product names, prices, images, and descriptions all come from your Adobe Commerce catalog. You do not create or edit individual product pages in Document Authoring — the boilerplate generates product URLs automatically from the catalog.

Your authoring work on a PDP is limited to three things: setting a `default-sku` on the document at `/products/default` so you can preview with real product data, adding companion blocks (such as product recommendations) to its layout, and changing the words shoppers see via [Labels and placeholders](/merchants/quick-start/labels-and-placeholders/). After the initial setup, you rarely need to return to this page.
</Aside>

You add the block to a different page only when you are building a custom landing page that highlights a specific product. See [Feature a product on a marketing page](#feature-a-product-on-a-marketing-page).

<Aside type="tip" title="Confirm the document at /products/default exists">
Open `/products/default` in your content folder in Document Authoring. If the document is missing or empty, the PDP cannot render in preview.
</Aside>

## How products become pages

If you have used a traditional content management system, you might expect to build a page for each product. You do not. On a Commerce storefront, products and product pages work differently:

- **You manage products in Commerce Admin, not in Document Authoring.** Add, edit, and remove products in the Adobe Commerce catalog. Their names, prices, images, descriptions, and options all live there.
- **Every product gets a page automatically.** As soon as a product is in your catalog, the boilerplate serves a live PDP for it at `/products/<url-key>/<sku>`, rendered by the same `product-details` block. You do not author these pages one by one.
- **You set up the `product-details` block once at `/products/default`.** Your work in Document Authoring on the PDP is a one-time setup: style the block, add companion blocks (such as product recommendations), and set the words shoppers see. After that, your ongoing product work happens in Commerce Admin, and the storefront reflects it without further authoring.

### View a real product page

To confirm a real product renders (not just the preview at `/products/default`):

<Steps>

1. Find a product's URL key and SKU in Commerce Admin under **Catalog → Products**, or simply open a category or search results page on your storefront and click any product — the link already points to its PDP.

1. Open the product URL, which follows the pattern `/products/<url-key>/<sku>` (for example, `/products/cool-hoodie/HOODIE-001`).

1. The PDP renders with that product's live catalog data. This is the page your shoppers see.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Unlike the document at `/products/default`, a real product URL works in a normal browser tab and through the paper-plane preview, because the SKU comes from the URL. The `/products/default` document needs the inline live-preview pane instead — see [Preview the PDP using default-sku](#preview-the-pdp-using-default-sku).
</Aside>

## What shoppers see

The PDP renders these regions, top to bottom on mobile and side-by-side on desktop. The numbers on the screenshot match the legend below.

</Diagram>

<Callouts>

1. **Image gallery**: thumbnails or a carousel. See [`ProductGallery`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-gallery/).
1. **Product header**: name and SKU. See [`ProductHeader`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-header/).
1. **Price**: list price, plus a sale price when one applies. See [`ProductPrice`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-price/).
1. **Short description**: a one- or two-line summary under the price. See [`ProductShortDescription`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-short-description/).
1. **Options**: size, color, or bundle items for configurable and bundle products. See [`ProductOptions`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-options/).
1. **Quantity selector**. See [`ProductQuantity`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-quantity/).
1. **Action buttons**: Add to Cart and Add to Wishlist. The block renders these, not the underlying <Term>drop-in</Term>, so a developer can rearrange or restyle them in the block code.
1. **Full description**: the long product description. See [`ProductDescription`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-description/).
1. **Additional attributes**: extra product attributes the catalog provides. See [`ProductAttributes`](/dropins/product-details/containers/product-attributes/).

</Callouts>

The `product-details` block is shared between B2C and B2B storefronts. For B2B-only experiences such as negotiable quotes, requisition lists, or purchase orders, use the matching B2B blocks listed in [B2B Commerce blocks](/merchants/blocks/b2b/).

### Property descriptions

#### default-sku

A product SKU the block uses when the page does not already supply one. The boilerplate reads `default-sku` only when the current page is the document at `/products/default` and the page is shown in the Document Authoring inline live-preview pane (the split-screen view inside the editor) or edited in Universal Editor. These are the preview modes that add the URL parameter the block looks for. The paper-plane Publish to Preview button does not trigger this path. On any other page, or on the live storefront, the block uses the `sku` page metadata first and then falls back to the SKU at the end of the URL path.

In practice, set `default-sku` on the document at `/products/default` so authors and reviewers can see a real product in preview. Setting it on other pages only has an effect on marketing pages that intentionally render the PDP outside the `/products/...` URL pattern.

### URL parameters the block reads

The `product-details` block also reacts to two URL search parameters. You do not set these in the block table. The boilerplate adds them when a shopper acts on the page:

- `itemUid` — switches the block into update mode for the cart line item with that ID. The add-to-cart button changes to an update button. This is set when a shopper clicks Edit on a cart line item.
- `optionsUIDs` — restores selected product options (for example, `size: large, color: blue`) when shoppers return from the wishlist or share a link.

Most authors do not need to think about these parameters. They explain why the PDP sometimes shows an Update button instead of Add to Cart.

### Preview the PDP using default-sku

At `/products/default`, the block reads a `default-sku` row only when the page is shown in the Document Authoring inline live-preview pane (the split-screen view inside the editor) or edited in Universal Editor — those are the two preview modes that add the parameter the block needs. With one of those methods, you can render real product data even though no SKU is in the URL. The paper-plane (Publish to Preview) button is a third kind of preview but does not work here; see the warning at the end of this procedure for why.

<Steps>

1. In Document Authoring, open the document at `/products/default`.

1. Add or update the `product-details` table with a `default-sku` row that points to a SKU in your catalog. To find a SKU, open Commerce Admin under **Catalog → Products**, or ask your integrator.

1. Use the **live-preview pane** inside the Document Authoring editor to see the PDP render. In the editor, click the split-screen icon on the right sidebar to open the inline preview. The PDP renders using the SKU you set.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="The paper-plane button does not work here">
The paper-plane (Publish to Preview) button at the top of the editor opens `https://main--{repo}--{org}.aem.page/products/default` in a new browser tab **without** the `?dapreview` parameter. Without that parameter, the PDP code cannot find a SKU — it ignores `default-sku` and redirects the tab to `/notfound`, which looks like a 404.

This is expected behavior, not a broken site. Use the **inline live-preview pane** in the editor instead. That pane loads the page with `?dapreview=...`, which tells the block to use `default-sku`.
</Aside>

The `default-sku` value is only used at `/products/default` and only in editor or preview mode. On a real product URL like `/products/cool-hoodie/HOODIE-001`, the SKU comes from the URL instead. You do not need to keep `default-sku` up to date with real catalog changes.

### Companion blocks for a PDP

A typical PDP includes more than just the `product-details` block. The most common companion authors add at `/products/default` is [Product Recommendations](/merchants/content-customizations/product-recommendations/), placed under the `product-details` block to surface "You may also like" and similar carousels. You can also add generic content blocks the boilerplate ships (for example, an `accordion` for an FAQ, or a `hero` banner) when your storefront design calls for them.

Each companion is a separate table in the same document. The `product-details` block does not need to know about the others; it just lives alongside them.

## Troubleshooting

In the browser developer tools console, two warnings point to common setup problems:

- `No defaultSku found in product-details block` — you opened `/products/default` in Universal Editor or Document Authoring preview but the `default-sku` row is missing from the `product-details` table. Add the row.
- `Unable to infer main image from JSON-LD or meta tags` — the page is missing the prerendered product metadata that the boilerplate uses to preload the hero image. Run the [metadata generation script](/setup/seo/metadata/#generate-metadata) for the catalog, or confirm the page metadata table includes a valid `sku`. The gallery still renders from the API; you only lose the preload.

### Clicking the paper-plane button at /products/default redirects to a 404

The paper-plane (Publish to Preview) button in the Document Authoring editor opens `https://main--{repo}--{org}.aem.page/products/default` in a new browser tab. That tab does not carry the `?dapreview` parameter that tells the PDP block to use the `default-sku` value from the document. Without a SKU — from the URL, from page metadata, or from `?dapreview` — the block redirects the browser to `/notfound`.

This is expected behavior. The document at `/products/default` is not meant to be opened directly in a browser. Use the **inline live-preview pane** inside the Document Authoring editor to preview it (click the split-screen icon on the right sidebar). That pane adds `?dapreview=...` to the URL, the block reads `default-sku`, and the PDP renders normally.

If the Add to Cart button stays disabled, the product has required options (such as size or color) that the shopper has not selected yet. The block enables the button when the option selection becomes valid.
