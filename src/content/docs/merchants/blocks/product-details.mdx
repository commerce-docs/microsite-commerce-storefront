The Product Details block renders the page that shoppers see when they view a single product: the image gallery, price, options, add-to-cart button, and more. Most shopper sessions go through the product page before a purchase, so it is one of the most important Commerce blocks on your site. Your storefront already includes this block, so you do not need to add it from scratch.

## What shoppers see

Product names, prices, images, and descriptions all come from your catalog, so you do not create or edit them here. Code controls the layout and what appears in each region, not the block table in Document Authoring.

## Preview a product

The `/products/default` document is shared by product pages. Follow [Preview a product](/how-tos/preview-product/) to choose a catalog SKU and view the template in the inline live-preview pane.

## View a real product page

Real product URLs normally identify the product using `/products/<url-key>/<sku>`. Use [Check a real product page](/how-tos/preview-product/#check-a-real-product-page) to verify the shopper-facing result outside the editor.

## Feature a product on a marketing page

Follow [Feature a product on a campaign page](/how-tos/feature-product-on-campaign-page/) when you need purchasing controls directly on a campaign page. Prefer linking to the existing product page when that meets your needs.

## Add companion blocks to a product page

Follow [Add supporting content to product pages](/how-tos/add-supporting-content-to-product-pages/) to place headings, images, or companion blocks around the product details. This changes the shared template. For content that should appear only for selected products or categories, use [Show enrichment content](/how-tos/show-enrichment-content/).
