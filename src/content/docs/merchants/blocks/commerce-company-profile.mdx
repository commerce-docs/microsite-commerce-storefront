Company users see their company's profile, and users with permission edit it, through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the parts of the company profile that their company role allows: the account information and legal address, the contacts (the company administrator and sales representative), and the payment and shipping methods available to the company.
- Edit the account information and legal address, if their company role allows it.
- Turn the company address book on or off, and allow or block custom shipping addresses. Only Company Administrators can change these settings. For what the address book does, see [Addresses](/merchants/blocks/commerce-addresses/#company-addresses-on-b2b-storefronts).

For details, see the [CompanyProfile container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-profile/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company` page. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
