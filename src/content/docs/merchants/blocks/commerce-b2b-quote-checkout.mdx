Buyers check out a negotiable quote through this block.

## What buyers can do

- Review the items and prices in the quote.
- See the quote's shipping address, and enter or choose a billing address.
- Choose a shipping method and a payment method.
- Accept the terms and conditions, if your store turns them on, and place the order.

## Requirements

Buyers open this block from a quote in the [Negotiable Quote](/merchants/blocks/commerce-b2b-negotiable-quote/) block, which only signed-in customers can use. Enable company features and negotiable quotes in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company** and **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable B2B Quote**.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/b2b/quote-checkout` page. Use this exact path, because the Negotiable Quote block opens it when a buyer checks out a quote. For the page title and metadata, see [Quote pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#quotes).

When the page loads, the block replaces the page title, description, and other meta tags with **B2B Checkout**. After the buyer places the order, it changes them to **B2B Order Confirmation**. These values come from the block code, so the page's `metadata` table cannot change them.

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `checkout`, `account`, and `quote-management` [placeholder sheets](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). These labels are set in the block code, so you cannot change them in a sheet: the **B2B Checkout** heading, the **Shipping address** title, the **Your Quote** heading and its **Edit** link, and the **Place Order** or **Place Purchase Order** button.

After the buyer places the order, the block shows the [Checkout Success](/merchants/blocks/commerce-checkout-success/) confirmation, whose order details use the `order` sheet. For a purchase order, it shows the [Purchase Order Checkout Success](/merchants/blocks/commerce-b2b-po-checkout-success/) confirmation instead.
