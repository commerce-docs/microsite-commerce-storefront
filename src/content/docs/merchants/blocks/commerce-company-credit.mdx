Company buyers view credit balances and transaction history through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See the company's credit limit, outstanding balance, and available credit.
- See the company's credit history, when **show-history** is `true`.

For details, see the [CompanyCredit container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-credit/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Company credit works with the **Payment on Account** payment method. Turn it on in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **Sales** > **Payment Methods** > **Payment on Account** > **Enabled**. Set each company's credit limit in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Company Credit**.

### Property descriptions

**show-history**: Controls whether to show company credit transaction history ('true') or only credit summary information ('false'). To enable this feature, set to `true`. Default: `true`.

### Important notes

- Requires user authentication. Unauthenticated users are automatically redirected to the login page.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company/credit` page. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).
