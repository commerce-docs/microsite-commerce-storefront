Shoppers request a new company account through this block.

## What buyers can do

- Fill in a form to register a new company account.
- After they submit, see a message that the request is being reviewed.

For details, see the [CompanyRegistration container](/dropins-b2b/company-management/containers/company-registration/).

## Requirements

Customers do not need to sign in to use this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Allow company registration in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **Customers** > **Company Configuration** > **General** > **Allow Company Registration from the Storefront**. When registration is turned off, the block sends signed-in customers to the account page and guests to the sign-in page. The block does the same when the `commerce-companies-enabled` setting in your storefront configuration is `false`. For this setting, see [Storefront configuration](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options). It also sends signed-in customers who already belong to a company to the account page.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/company/create` page. Use this exact path, because the Company Structure block links to it when a customer does not belong to a company yet. For the page title and metadata, see [Company management pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#company-management).

## Labels and messages

Most of the block's labels and messages come from the `company` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/). The **Male**, **Female**, and **Not Specified** options in the gender field are set in the drop-in code, so you cannot change them in a sheet.
