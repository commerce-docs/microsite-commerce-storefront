Add this block to the purchase orders page so buyers can find their own purchase orders and check each one's status. The block has no settings to fill in.

## Page setup

Place this block first among the purchase order lists so buyers can find their own purchases before reviewing other orders.

<Steps>

1. Open the document for `/customer/purchase-orders` in your authoring tool.
1. Add a one-cell block table after Account Header and before Purchase Orders Requiring Approval.

</Steps>

Use this block name in the table's only cell:

<TableWrapper>

| commerce-b2b-po-customer-purchase-orders |
| --- |

</TableWrapper>

Use this exact page path, because other blocks link to it. For the layout and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Configuration

Leave the block table with just the block name. This block has no settings; manage access through the requirements below.

## Requirements

Ask your Commerce administrator or developer to confirm these settings before you preview the page.

<TableWrapper nowrap={[0]}>

| Where | What to set up |
| ----- | -------------- |
| Commerce Admin | Turn on company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. |
| Commerce Admin | Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. |
| Commerce Admin | For each company, turn on purchase orders in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. |
| Storefront configuration | Set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. Developers manage this setting in `config.json` or the Config Service. |
| Company administrator's storefront account | In **Roles and Permissions**, give the **Order Approvals** and **View my Purchase Orders** permissions to the roles of customers who use this block. The company administrator has full permissions by default. |

</TableWrapper>

## Check the page

Preview the page before publishing to check that the block is available to the intended buyers.

<Steps>

1. Preview `/customer/purchase-orders`.
1. Sign in as the company administrator or as a customer whose role has both permissions listed above, in a company with purchase orders turned on.
1. Check that the customer's purchase order list appears.

</Steps>

If the storefront sends you to the sign-in page, sign in before checking the block. If it sends you to the account page, ask your developer to check `commerce-b2b-enabled` and your company administrator to check **Order Approvals**. If the page opens but this block is missing, ask your company administrator to check **View my Purchase Orders**. Company administrators do not need these role permissions.

## What buyers can do

- See their own purchase orders, 10, 20, or 30 per page (10 by default).
- Open a purchase order's details.

For details, see the [CustomerPurchaseOrders container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/customer-purchase-orders/).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
