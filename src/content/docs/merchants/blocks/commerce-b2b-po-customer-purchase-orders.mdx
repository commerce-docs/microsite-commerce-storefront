Buyers see their own purchase orders, with each one's status, through this block.

## What buyers can do

- See their own purchase orders, 10, 20, or 30 per page (10 by default).
- Open a purchase order's details.

For details, see the [CustomerPurchaseOrders container](/dropins-b2b/purchase-order/containers/customer-purchase-orders/).

## Requirements

Customers must log in to access this block. Enable company features in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Enable Company**. Turn on purchase orders in **Stores** > **Settings** > **Configuration** > **General** > **B2B Features** > **Order Approval Configuration** > **Enable Purchase Orders**, and then for each company in **Customers** > **Companies** > edit the company > **Advanced Settings** > **Enable Purchase Orders**. In your storefront configuration, set [`commerce-b2b-enabled`](/setup/configuration/commerce-configuration/#additional-configuration-options) to `true`. If it's missing or `false`, the storefront sends customers to their account page. Customers need the **Order Approvals** permission in their company role, or the storefront sends them to their account page. The block appears only for customers who also have **View my Purchase Orders**. Company Administrators don't need these permissions.

## Configuration

No configurations available for this block.

## Page setup

Add this block to the `/customer/purchase-orders` page, together with the other purchase order list blocks. Use this exact path, because other blocks link to it. For the block order, layout, and page metadata, see [Purchase order pages](/merchants/blocks/b2b/pages/#purchase-orders).

## Labels and messages

The block's labels and messages come from the `purchase-order` [placeholder sheet](/merchants/blocks/placeholder-sheets/#b2b-drop-in-sheets). To change them, see [Labels and placeholders](/how-tos/change-labels-and-placeholders/).
