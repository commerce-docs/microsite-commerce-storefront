Prerendered product pages are generated and published before customers visit them. The system creates and stores each page in advance, delivering it instantly when requested.

<Aside type="note" title="Developer Setup Required">
Your development team configures the prerender system. Once set up, it runs automatically with no changes to your workflow.
</Aside>

## What you need to do

**Nothing different.** Manage your products in Adobe Commerce as usual. The prerender system works automatically in the background.

## Why it matters for your store

Prerendered pages improve your store's performance and visibility:

- **Better search rankings:** Search engines index your product information immediately. Your products appear in search results faster, helping shoppers find your store.
- **Faster page loads:** Pages load instantly, improving:
  - Customer retention
  - Product views
  - Conversion rates
  - Mobile experience
- **Performance impact:** Most product pages load in under one second.
- **AI and social media ready:** Product links on social media display your images, descriptions, and pricing automatically. AI shopping assistants can understand and recommend your products.

## How the system works

<Steps>

1. You add, update, or delete products in Adobe Commerce.
2. The system detects changes automatically.
3. Product pages generate and publish to Edge Delivery Services in the background.
4. Updates (or removals) are visible in your storefront within minutes.

</Steps>

## Product lifecycle

### Previewing draft products

Products only appear on your storefront once they are active and visible in your catalog. You can preview the product template (with a `defaultSku`) in your authoring environment to review the page layout and content before any products go live.

### Scheduling product launches

Products appear on your storefront only after they are activated in the Commerce environment. If you schedule a product to activate on a future date in Adobe Commerce, the product page will automatically go live on your storefront within approximately one hour of that date arriving. No additional steps are required from you.

### Removing offline products

When a product's offline date passes or it is disabled in Adobe Commerce, it is automatically removed from your published storefront within approximately one hour. You do not need to take any manual action to unpublish it.

## What content is prerendered

Prerendered pages include:

- Product names and descriptions
- Product images and image carousels
- SEO metadata (titles, descriptions, keywords)
- Structured data for search engines
- Product categories and attributes

:::note
Prices, stock quantities, and personalized recommendations load separately to ensure customers always see current data.
:::
